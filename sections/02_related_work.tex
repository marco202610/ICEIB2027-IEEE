\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
Activity recording services document exercise using devices and location data \cite{strava}, but the present research examines a broader decision-support workflow incorporating user needs, route constraints, and environmental information. We do not claim that existing products lack all such capabilities.

Food-image estimation is another relevant area. The study in \cite{foodstudy} illustrates the uncertainty of estimating nutrition from images using general-purpose models. Our proposed workflow therefore treats image interpretation as an input to user confirmation and database-based calculation, rather than as a validated measurement. Whether this workflow improves accuracy or reduces burden requires comparative testing.

The source project also describes a separate encrypted sensor-data prototype \cite{blockchain}. Hash comparisons can detect changes relative to a trusted digest; they do not establish that sensor readings are authentic, accurate, or clinically meaningful. Our work combines these directions in a proposed architecture; integration and effectiveness remain open research questions.
