\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
Smartphone-based activity services can record location and exercise data \cite{strava}; GIS and route services provide geographic context. Our proposed workflow combines these capabilities with user-selected POIs and evaluates the trade-off between sensing accuracy and resource use. This comparison does not assert that existing services lack these features.

Dietary estimation also requires care. A study of food-image estimates by general-purpose models reported substantial energy-estimation errors \cite{foodstudy}. Our proposed food MCP workflow retrieves food data and asks the user to confirm items and portions; its accuracy and usability remain to be evaluated.

A separate sensor-data prototype uses encryption and digest verification \cite{blockchain}. A digest comparison checks consistency against a trusted reference but does not prove the original measurements are genuine. Further peer-reviewed sources are needed for smartphone positioning, step estimation, energy expenditure and phone-based heart-rate sensing.
