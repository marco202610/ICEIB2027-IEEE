\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented a proposed AIoT fitness-coaching architecture that combines contextual sensing, agent-coordinated tools, human-confirmed dietary assessment, and multimodal interaction. A separate sensor-data prototype provides preliminary local and simulated development evidence, while real blockchain anchoring and end-to-end system integration remain unfinished. Future work will implement and evaluate the complete workflow against defined baselines and investigate privacy, usability, and robustness. Claims about exercise safety and sustained participation require independent empirical evidence.
