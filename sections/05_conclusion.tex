\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We proposed a mobile web fitness workflow combining AI-avatar route planning, phone sensing, GIS interaction, food MCP queries and consented data management. The first phase focuses on trajectory, distance, speed, conditional energy estimates and the cost of lightweight collection. Real-world accuracy, food integration, cloud deployment and blockchain transactions remain to be evaluated. Heart-rate sensing and related cardiac workload are reserved for a second phase; no clinical or exercise-safety benefit is established by the proposed architecture.
